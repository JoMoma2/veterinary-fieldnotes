## What was actually said

The first synthetic shift ends with pending results and no stated outcome. A later teaching exercise proposes organizing a note by observations, completed actions, suspicions, and unanswered questions.

## Proposed format

1. Preserve the presenting account and its source.
2. List actions that the discussion says occurred.
3. Label concerns as suspicions until supported.
4. Make pending questions explicit.
5. Write “unknown” for an unstated diagnosis or outcome.

## Limits of this example

This is an educational note format, not a hospital policy. The later discussion supplies no new patient facts and does not establish that a real clinician accepted a handover task.
