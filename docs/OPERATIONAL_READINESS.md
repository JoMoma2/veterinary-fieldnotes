## GitHub/local storage policy update

Completed knowledge stays in the local transactional library and is committed/pushed to the private master when `[github].enabled=true`. Each `run`/`nightly` makes at most one sync attempt; failures persist in `WORKSPACE/master-sync/state.json` and class-only `events.jsonl`, with 60-second initial retry delay capped at one hour. Subsequent invocations retry even with no new recordings, without repeating inference. Unchanged successful snapshots are remotely reverified periodically. Repeated failures stay pending rather than discarding work. Non-fast-forward errors require reconciliation; no force push or automatic reset is used.

The service template no longer requires a backup key/rclone configuration. It remains **uninstalled and disabled**. Optional backups are requested explicitly; missing independent-provider credentials cannot prevent ordinary processing or GitHub sync. Real storage requires `[github].allow_clinical=true` **and** `[backup].allow_clinical_remote=true` after authorization; both remain false by default. Public publication approvals remain separate.
