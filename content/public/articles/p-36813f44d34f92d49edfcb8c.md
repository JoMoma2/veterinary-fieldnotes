## Source addition · 2026-11-11

The staff member explained that the liquid amoxiclav for this 3.5 kg Shih Tzu comes in a syringe with a fill line marked on it, and the volume is 'a very tiny amount.' The recommended primary technique is to get the medication directly into the dog's mouth. If the dog resists, the fallback is to pour the medication over a very small amount of wet food so that the full dose is consumed in one lick or bite. The explicit priority stated is 'we want to get the full dose inside them,' meaning partial consumption (e.g., the dog licks some food and spits the rest) is inadequate. The syringe technique involves flipping it upside down and drawing up to the fill line (b0190–b0191). No specific millilitre volume was stated in the transcript.
