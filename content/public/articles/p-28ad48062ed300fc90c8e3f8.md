## Source addition · 2026-11-11

The cat's blood work (described as a 'small blood work panel' and a 'Nova Blood Gas') did not reveal an overtly abnormal finding. The team explicitly stated the differential was between 'some toxin or some disease within the brain' and acknowledged 'we're just not sure at this point.' The cat's owners could not identify a specific toxin. No advanced diagnostics (imaging, CSF analysis, specific toxin screens) were performed or discussed as completed. The discharge plan included empiric Kepra, restricted activity, monitoring, and a follow-up with the primary veterinarian in 2-3 days for 'long-term treatment options.' The underlying etiology remained unresolved at discharge. A later phone update confirmed the cat had vomited prior to arrival but was otherwise stable, with no change in status.
